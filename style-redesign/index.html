AquaGuard — Flood Rescue Platform

Founder Active

University of Technology Sydney · Sep 2025 — Present

  • Founded a real-time flood emergency coordination platform; led cross-functional team to MVP in 6 months.
  • 1st Place at EPICS 8th (ASU, 2026) · 1,500+ followers · ~1M content views

AquaGuard is a real-time emergency dispatch platform inspired by Uber and Grab's coordination model. Instead of treating emergencies as isolated SOS requests, AquaGuard orchestrates citizens, rescue teams, and government coordinators on a shared operational platform, enabling live incident reporting, mission dispatch, and resource coordination. Initially built for flood response, it lays the foundation for a scalable emergency coordination infrastructure, now deployed at aquaguard.vn.
