About Me

Hi! I'm Nguyen Minh Quan.

I am an undergraduate student in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS) and Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT), Vietnam.

I am currently a member of the URA Research Group at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology, where I participate in research projects involving machine learning, explainable artificial intelligence (XAI), and human-centered technology. My recent work includes model interpretability using SHAP, transformer-based architectures, and real-time AI systems for practical applications.

Beyond research, I am the Founder and Team Leader of AquaGuard, a flood rescue and emergency response platform designed to connect victims, volunteers, and rescue teams through web and mobile technologies. The project integrates artificial intelligence, geospatial technologies, and community-driven coordination to improve disaster response and resilience.

Outside of technical work, I am actively involved in leadership, public speaking, and community initiatives. I have served as a student leader, event coordinator, master of ceremonies (MC), and project lead across academic, social-impact, and international collaboration programs. I am particularly interested in the intersection of technology, education, and social innovation, with a focus on developing solutions that create meaningful societal impact.

My current research interests include Computer Vision, Human Action Recognition, Transformer Architectures, and Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI). I am particularly interested in exploring how transformer-based models can improve human activity understanding while providing greater transparency and interpretability in real-world applications.
