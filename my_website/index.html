About Me

Hi! I'm Nguyen Minh Quan.

I am an undergraduate student in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS) and Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT), Vietnam.

I am currently a member of the URA Research Group at HCMUT, working on research involving machine learning, explainable AI (XAI), and human-centered technology. My recent work focuses on transformer-based models for human action recognition, with SHAP-based interpretability analysis.

I founded AquaGuard, a flood rescue platform that connects citizens and rescue teams through real-time technology — web and mobile apps, live dashboards, and AI-powered coordination. Beyond technical work, I lead teams across academic and social-impact programs, with a focus on technology that creates meaningful societal value.

My research interests lie at the intersection of Human-Centered AI, Computer Vision, and Explainable Machine Learning. I am working toward building intelligent systems that are transparent, trustworthy, and deployable in real-world settings — particularly in healthcare, public safety, and disaster response.
