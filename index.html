01 ABOUT

A builder who
thinks like a researcher.

I’m an AI student at UTS, studying in Ho Chi Minh City through HCMUT’s trans­na­tion­al programme. What interests me most is the space between research and busi­ness: how an idea from the lab becomes something people actually use.

On the research side, I work at HCMUT’s URA Research Group on explainable models for early action an­tic­i­pa­tion. On the busi­ness side, I scout early-stage startups at B71 Vietnam and lead AquaGuard, a climate-tech startup tackling flood re­sponse in Vietnam.

I also host HCMUT’s in­ter­na­tion­al events in English and Vietnamese. I’d rather show the messy middle of how something got built than a tidy list of what shipped.
