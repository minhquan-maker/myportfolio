AI/ML Engineer · Solo project

Leveraging 3D Skeleton Motion for Early Action Anticipation via Spatio-Temporal Causal Modeling

Manhtuan Nguyen☉ , Duong T. T. Tran ☉ , Quan M. Nguyen☉ , Quan Hac☉ , Tho T. Quan☉ URA Research Group · HCMUT, VNU-HCM

3D Skeleton Early Action Anticipation Causal Modeling CTSK-Former

Abstract

  • Problem. Predict the next action before the current one completes — most skeleton methods only recognise what's happening now and ignore prior history.
  • Model — CTSK-Former. Prefix-safe spatio-temporal encoder + motion-conditioned dual-stream history reasoner fusing cross-attentive & masked-mean summaries through ratio-aware gates.
  • Robustness. Curriculum History Corruption + consistency regularisation handle noisy online histories.
  • Setup. PKU-MMD Phase 1, XView camera-M protocol — both oracle and predicted-history regimes.
  • Result. 90.17% Mean EAA with predicted history — +6.25 pp over single-stream HAPS-Former, with a +9.07 pp spike at the earliest observation ratio.

Conclusions

  • CTSK-Former sets a strong controlled baseline for next-action anticipation from partial motion + discrete history — robust to realistic history-rollout errors.
  • Biggest wins land at low observation ratios, proving historical context carries the model when motion evidence is thin.
  • Future work: more datasets, per-component ablations, and extension to elderly activity anticipation for healthcare-grade activity understanding.
